# [network] Revenge of the System Administrator

> **Category:** `network`  
> **CTF:** KubSTU CTF 2026 Spring

---

 

A scandal is brewing at an ordinary school. The system administrator, who had worked for just under a year for pennies, suddenly quit, leaving behind a strange note: *"If you think saving money on my nerves will benefit the school — check your accounts. I left the door open for those who know how to look."* The principal is panicking: the accounting department claims that access to the financial reporting server is blocked and all passwords have been changed. You are a hired cybersecurity specialist. Your task is to infiltrate the school's network, reconstruct the chain of events, and find evidence of embezzlement.
