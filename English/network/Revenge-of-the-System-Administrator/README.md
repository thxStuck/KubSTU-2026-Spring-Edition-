A scandal is brewing at an ordinary school. The system administrator, who worked for almost a year for peanuts, suddenly quit, leaving behind a strange note:
"If you think that saving money on my nerves will benefit the school — check your accounts. I left a door open for those who know how to look."

The principal is in a panic: accounting claims that access to the financial reporting server is blocked and all passwords have been changed. You are an invited cybersecurity specialist. Your task is to penetrate the school's network, reconstruct the chain of events, and find evidence of embezzlement.
